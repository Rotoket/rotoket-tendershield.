**Настройки в `.env`:**
```env

TENDER_SMTP_PORT=587
TENDER_SMTP_USER=your-email@gmail.com
TENDER_SMTP_PASSWORD=your-app-password
TENDER_SMTP_FROM=noreply@tendershield.pro
```
